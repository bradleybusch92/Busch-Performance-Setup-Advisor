Additional tuning notes

Pullbar rate: Tune pullbar rate to your motor, your specific track conditions, and your driver. If the car is busting the tires loose when you apply throttle, soften the pull bar rate. If the car is not busting the tires loose but is just not going forward, stiffen the pull bar rate.

Car feels on top of the track: This is likely a timing issue where weight is not being transferred into the RR tire patch early enough. Move more ballast toward the RR corner or soften the compression valving of the RR shock.
